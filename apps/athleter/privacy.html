1. Who We Are

AthleteR is provided by [Company Legal Name], [Country/State of Incorporation] ("we", "us", "our"). For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws, we act as the data controller for the account and billing information described in Section 2, and as a data processor on behalf of the coach (the controller) for athlete performance data described in Section 3.

2. Information We Collect About You (the Coach)

  • Account information: name and email address, collected when you sign in — directly, or via Google Sign-In / Sign in with Apple if you choose one of those sign-in methods (in that case, the sign-in provider shares your name and email with us; we never see your password).
  • Usage and diagnostic data: basic technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the Service (e.g., device type, app version, crash logs), collected via our infrastructure provider (Google Firebase).
  • Subscription status: whether you have an active subscription and which tier, managed through RevenueCat, which validates purchases made via the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

3. Athlete Data You Enter (Processed on Your Behalf)

As part of using the Service, you enter data about the athletes you coach: names, dates of birth, physical measurements, test results, sport-specific assessments, discipline notes, and optionally a profile photograph. This data is processed by us solely as your processor, on your instructions, to provide the Service to you. You, the coach, are the data controller for this athlete data. You are responsible for having an appropriate legal basis (such as a coaching agreement with a club or school, or applicable parental/guardian consent) to collect and process it, and for responding to any data subject requests from athletes or their guardians under applicable law.

4. Minors' Data

Many athletes tracked in the Service are minors. Athletes never have their own account or independent access to the Service; all data about them is entered and controlled by you, the coach. If you are subject to laws requiring parental or guardian consent before recording a minor's data (which vary by country, for example GDPR's provisions on information society services offered to children, or similar rules elsewhere), you are responsible for obtaining and documenting that consent through your own club, school, or organizational processes before entering the athlete's data.

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where GDPR applies, we process your account data under Article 6(1)(b) (processing necessary to perform our contract with you, i.e., to provide the Service) and Article 6(1)(f) (our legitimate interest in maintaining and securing the Service). We process athlete data as your processor, under a data processing agreement implied by these Terms, on the legal basis you as controller have established with the athlete or their guardian.

6. How We Use Information

We use the information described above only to: provide, maintain, and improve the Service; synchronize your data across your devices and with organizations you explicitly share it with; provide customer support; and comply with legal obligations. We do not use your data or athlete data for advertising, and we do not sell it to third parties.

7. Where Data Is Stored and International Transfers

Data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Google Cloud infrastructure), in the region configured for this project. Where data is transferred outside your own country (for example, to a Google Cloud region in another jurisdiction), we rely on the transfer safeguards Google Firebase itself provides (such as Standard Contractual Clauses, where applicable) to ensure an adequate level of protection.

8. Who Else Sees Your Data

Athlete data is visible only to coaches you explicitly invite into a shared organization with that athlete. It is never sold, and never shared with advertisers or data brokers. Our infrastructure providers (Google Firebase, Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, RevenueCat, Apple, Google) process data on our behalf under their own data processing terms, solely to provide the underlying infrastructure the Service runs on.

9. Data Retention

We retain your account data and the athlete data you enter for as long as your account remains active, plus a reasonable period afterward to allow account recovery and to meet legal retention obligations. You can permanently delete your account and personal data at any time (Settings → Account → Delete Account); deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription, which is managed separately through the App Store or Google Play.

10. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit and access controls provided by our infrastructure provider. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, and we cannot guarantee against every possible security incident, particularly those originating outside our systems (such as a compromised device or a reused password). If we become aware of a security incident affecting your personal data, we will notify you and any relevant regulator as required by applicable law.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, under GDPR if you are in the EU/EEA/UK), you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, object to, or delete your personal data, and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app (Settings → Backup & Restore for export, Settings → Account → Delete Account for deletion) or by contacting us at the address below.

12. Children's Privacy (Coach Accounts)

The Service is intended for use by coaches who are adults. We do not knowingly collect account registration information directly from children. Athlete data about minors is entered by their coach, as described in Sections 3 and 4 above, not collected directly from the minor.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be presented for your acceptance through the same in-app flow used for updates to our Terms of Service.
