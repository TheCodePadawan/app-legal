Data Processing Agreement (DPA)

Draft from the AthleteR app, modeled on the free DPA template published by the LfDI Baden-Württemberg (Germany's relevant supervisory authority for the company behind this app) and translated for convenience — the German version (avv-de.html) is the legally controlling text. This agreement only governs the processing relationship between you (coach, controller) and us (processor); it does not replace the legal basis you yourself need to collect athlete data (see Section 4), and it has not been reviewed by a lawyer — have it reviewed before relying on it, particularly because this involves special category data about minors.

1. Subject Matter and Duration

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") supplements the Terms of Service between you ("coach", "controller") and [Company Legal Name] ("we", "processor"), and governs our processing of personal data about athletes you enter into the Service, under Art. 28 GDPR. It applies for the life of your AthleteR account and ends automatically when the account is deleted, subject to Section 10.

2. Nature and Purpose of Processing

We process athlete data solely to provide the Service to you: storage, cross-device sync, computing performance and comparison metrics (including the BMI-for-age classification described in Section 3), display within the app, and — only when you explicitly initiate it — sharing with other coaches in the same organization. We do not determine the purposes or essential means of this processing; you do, by choosing which athletes, which data, and which features you use.

3. Categories of Data Subjects and Data

Data subjects: athletes you coach, a significant proportion of whom are minors.

Categories of personal data:

  • Core profile data: name, date of birth, position, assigned groups/organizations;
  • an optional profile photo;
  • special category personal data (Art. 9 GDPR): physical measurements (height, weight, and other optional anthropometric values) and the BMI-for-age classification computed from them — a deterministic classification against the publicly published CDC growth-chart methodology, not a machine learning output. This is health data within the meaning of Art. 4(15) GDPR. The classification is computed only from values you enter yourself, and is only shown when you view it in the app — we do not independently trigger this processing, and we do not decide for which athlete or occasion you use it;
  • sport-specific test/assessment results, coaching notes, goals;
  • discipline/behavioral notes, where you choose to record them.

4. Instructions

We process athlete data only on your documented instructions. Those instructions are expressed through your use of the app's features (which data you enter, which features you enable, who you share with) and this agreement; they exclude any instruction that would violate GDPR or other applicable data protection law — if we believe an instruction does, we will inform you without undue delay. You alone are responsible for having a valid legal basis to collect and process athlete data, including effective guardian consent where required by applicable law for minors (see also our Privacy Policy, Section 4). This DPA only governs how we process data on your behalf — it does not give you a legal basis you don't already have.

5. Confidentiality

Anyone with access to athlete data on our side, or on behalf of us, is bound by confidentiality. At our current size, that is the service operator personally; the same obligation will apply equally to any future employee or contractor with access.

6. Technical and Organizational Measures (TOMs)

The technical and organizational measures we take under Art. 32 GDPR are documented in a separate TOMs annex, available on request at the address in Section 14. We will notify you of any change to those measures that materially reduces the level of protection.

7. Sub-processors

You grant us general authorization to engage the following sub-processors, which provide the technical infrastructure the Service runs on:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud Platform (data storage, sync, authentication, crash diagnostics);
  • RevenueCat (subscription purchase validation — never receives athlete data);
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple (optional sign-in methods, only if you choose them).

We will notify you before adding or replacing a sub-processor; you may object for a substantiated, data-protection-related reason. A data protection agreement providing a level of protection equivalent to this DPA is in place with each sub-processor.

8. Assistance with Data Subject Rights

If an athlete or their guardian contacts us directly with an Art. 15-22 GDPR request, we will forward it to you, since you are the controller for that data. We will assist you, to the extent reasonably possible, in responding — in particular, the app already provides self-service tools (data export under Settings → Backup & Restore, deletion of an individual athlete or your entire personal roster under Settings → Data Management).

9. Breach Notification and DPIA Assistance

If we become aware of a personal data breach affecting athlete data, we will notify you without undue delay so you can meet your own Art. 33/34 GDPR obligations. On request, we will provide the information about our processing needed for a Data Protection Impact Assessment (Art. 35 GDPR).

10. Deletion and Return After Termination

After this agreement ends (in particular, when your account is deleted), we delete all athlete data processed on your behalf within the period stated in our Privacy Policy, unless a legal retention obligation requires otherwise. You can export your data at any time before deletion via the in-app export feature.

11. Audit Rights

We will provide the information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this agreement and permit reasonable audits, including inspections, on reasonable notice. At our current size, this is typically satisfied through documentation rather than an on-site audit.

12. Liability

Liability for GDPR infringements follows Art. 82 GDPR and the liability limitations in our Terms of Service, Section 12, to the extent those remain valid under mandatory data protection law.

13. Final Provisions

In case of conflict between this DPA and the Terms of Service, this DPA prevails with respect to the processing of athlete personal data. The general provisions in our Terms of Service, Section 17, otherwise apply. The German version of this DPA is the controlling text.
