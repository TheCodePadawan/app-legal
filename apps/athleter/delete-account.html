Delete your AthleteR account

Last updated: [Date]

AthleteR accounts are for coaches. You can permanently delete your account and associated personal data using the steps below. This page is provided for Google Play and other store account-deletion requirements.

1. Delete your account in the app (recommended)

  1. Open AthleteR and sign in to the coach account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Settings → Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.
  4. If prompted, re-enter your password (email/password accounts) or complete Google / Apple sign-in again so we can verify it is you.

Deletion is permanent. If you own a shared organization that still has other members, transfer ownership or delete that organization first; the app will refuse to delete your account until other members’ shared data would not be affected.

2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot use the in-app flow, email [contact email] from the email address associated with your AthleteR account. Include:

  • Subject line: AthleteR account deletion request
  • The email used for your AthleteR login
  • A clear statement that you want your account and personal data deleted

We may ask you to confirm ownership of the account before completing the request.

3. What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

  • Your coach login (Firebase Authentication account)
  • Your coach profile and profile photo (if any)
  • Athlete data, groups, and records stored under your personal (non-shared) data
  • Organizations you solely own (no other members)
  • Your membership in shared organizations you do not own (you leave; the org and other members’ data remain)

4. What is retained or not deleted by this flow

  • Shared organization data owned by others — athlete rosters and records in orgs you do not own stay with that organization and its remaining members.
  • Legal / security records — we may retain limited information where required by law, to resolve disputes, or to prevent abuse, for as long as those obligations apply.
  • Backups — deleted data may remain in encrypted backups for a short period until those backups rotate, then is purged in the normal backup lifecycle.

5. Timeline

  • In-app deletion: your account and deletable cloud data are removed immediately after you confirm (subject to the organization rules above).
  • Email requests: we aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

6. Subscriptions

Deleting your AthleteR account does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel billing separately in the store subscription settings for your device account.

See also the Privacy Policy for how AthleteR handles coach and athlete data.
