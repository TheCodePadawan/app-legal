Delete data in AthleteR (keep your account)

App: AthleteR Publisher: [Company Legal Name]

Overview

You can remove some or all of the athlete and coaching data stored in AthleteR without deleting your coach account. Use this when you want to clear a roster, remove a photo, or erase specific records but keep signing in.

How to request / delete data

  1. Open the AthleteR app and sign in.
  2. Open the athlete, group, or record you want to remove (from your roster, athlete profile, history screens, or related tools).
  3. Use the in-app delete / remove action for that item and confirm.
  4. Repeat for any other athletes or records you want gone. Deleted cloud-synced items are removed from your account’s data in our systems when sync completes.

If you cannot use the app, email [contact email] from your account email, subject “AthleteR data deletion”, and tell us which data to remove (for example: a named athlete, all athletes, or specific record types).

Types of data you can delete this way

  • Athlete profiles (names, dates of birth, sport/position, etc.)
  • Profile photographs
  • Performance tests, physical measurements, skill assessments, notes, goals, custom program records, and similar coaching data you entered
  • Groups / organization-linked roster items you control, where the app allows removal

What is kept when you only delete data

  • Your coach account (sign-in, name, email) remains until you delete the account separately.
  • Subscription / purchase status with Google Play or the App Store is unchanged (managed in the store, not by deleting roster data).
  • Short-lived backups or logs may linger for a reasonable period (typically up to about 30 days) for recovery and security, then are cleared in normal cleanup.

Export before you delete

Optional: use Settings → Backup & Restore or an athlete’s export / PDF / CSV actions to save a copy before removing data.
