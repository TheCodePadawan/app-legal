Parental/Guardian Consent Template

Last updated: [Date]

Draft, not legal advice. This template exists to make it easier for you to gather your own legal basis for recording athlete data (see the DPA, Section 4: that's your responsibility as coach, not ours). It doesn't replace review by your own club, school, or lawyer — requirements for valid consent for a minor vary by country and by the child's age. Adapt this template to your own situation before using it.

1. Who this is for

Coaches using AthleteR to record data about a minor athlete who want or need documented guardian consent for doing so. Not needed if your club or school already runs an equivalent consent process — a reference to AthleteR within that existing process is usually enough.

2. Template (print or copy)

Consent to Record Athlete Data in AthleteR

Coach/Club: ______________________________
Athlete (name): ______________________________
Athlete's date of birth: ______________________________

The coach/club named above uses the AthleteR app to record training data about the athletes they coach. The following data may be recorded about my/our child: name, date of birth, athletic test results, sport-specific assessments, coaching notes, and optionally a profile photograph.

Separately, since this is a special category of personal data (health data, GDPR Art. 9): the app can compute an age-referenced BMI classification from entered physical measurements (e.g. height, weight). This is an informational training aid, not a medical diagnosis.

I/we understand that I/we can withdraw this consent at any time by contacting the coach/club named above, and that deletion of the data can be requested through them.

Parent/Guardian name: ______________________________
Date: ______________________________
Signature: ______________________________

3. Usage notes

This template only governs the relationship between you (coach/ club) and the guardian — it doesn't change your own relationship with AthleteR, which is still governed by our DPA. Keep signed copies per your club/ school's own record-retention rules — AthleteR itself does not store this consent form.
